Help & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pet care services, appointments, and wellness standards.

How can I book a service or appointment?

You can effortlessly book online through our platform, choose your desired service, pick a convenient date and time, and receive instant confirmation.

What essential vaccinations does my pet need?

Core vaccinations include Rabies and DHPP for dogs, and Rabies and FVRCP for cats. Our veterinarians provide a customized vaccination schedule based on your pet's lifestyle.

How often should I bathe and groom my pet?

Most dogs benefit from professional grooming every 4 to 6 weeks depending on breed and coat. Regular brushing at home keeps coats healthy between spa sessions.

How often should I take my pet for a wellness checkup?

We recommend annual wellness exams for healthy adult pets and bi-annual checkups for puppies, kittens, and senior pets to detect any health issues early.
